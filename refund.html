Refund Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Because our services are digital and may be customized, refund eligibility depends on the order’s progress and delivery status.

1. Before work begins

If you cancel before work starts, you may request a full refund, less any non-refundable processing fees where permitted.

2. After work begins

Completed work and time already spent are generally non-refundable. A partial refund may be considered for the undelivered portion.

3. Completed services

Completed and delivered services are generally non-refundable. We will first attempt to correct any verified issue that falls within the confirmed scope.

4. Non-delivery or material failure

If we cannot deliver the agreed service, or the result materially differs from the confirmed scope and cannot be corrected within a reasonable period, a full or partial refund may be approved.

5. Customer responsibilities

Refunds may not be available when delivery is prevented because required access, information, files, instructions, or feedback were not supplied by the customer.

6. Request period

Contact us within 7 calendar days of delivery or the expected delivery date. Include your name, order details, payment reference, and issue description.

7. Processing

Approved refunds are submitted to the original payment method. Posting time depends on the payment provider and the customer’s bank or wallet provider.
