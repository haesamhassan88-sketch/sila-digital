Refund Policy

Last updated: 3 August 2026

Because our services are digital and often customized, refund eligibility depends on the progress and delivery status of the order.

1. Before work begins

If you cancel before work has started, you may request a full refund, less any non-refundable payment processing fees where permitted.

2. After work begins

Once work has started, completed work and time already spent are generally non-refundable. A partial refund may be considered for the undelivered portion of the service.

3. Completed or delivered services

Completed and delivered digital services are generally non-refundable. We will first attempt to correct any verified issue that falls within the agreed service scope.

4. Non-delivery or material service failure

If we are unable to deliver the agreed service, or if the delivered service materially differs from the confirmed scope and cannot be corrected within a reasonable period, the customer may be eligible for a full or partial refund.

5. Customer responsibilities

Refunds may not be available when delivery is delayed or prevented because the customer did not provide required information, access, files, feedback, or accurate instructions.

6. Refund requests

Contact us within 7 calendar days of delivery or the expected delivery date. Include your name, order details, payment reference, and a clear description of the issue.

7. Processing time

Approved refunds are submitted to the original payment method. The time needed for the amount to appear depends on the payment provider and the customer’s bank or wallet provider.
